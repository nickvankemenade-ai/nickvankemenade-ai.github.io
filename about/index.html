About

I'm an independent machine learning researcher. My work focuses on neural networks, and rather than specialising in one area, I work across the field.

Much of my research has been about representation: what convolutional object detectors and variational autoencoders learn internally, including my work on ConCoS. I've also trained GPT-style language models and latent diffusion models for image generation.

I work outside a university or lab. I publish my research on Zenodo and share code on GitHub.
