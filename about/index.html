About

I'm an independent machine learning researcher. I study how neural networks learn, and how they represent knowledge.

I don't specialise in one area, but rather work across the field. Much of my research has been about representation: what convolutional object detectors and variational autoencoders learn internally, including my work on ConCoS. I've also trained GPT-style language models and latent diffusion models for image generation.

I work outside a university or lab. I publish my research on Zenodo and share code on GitHub.
